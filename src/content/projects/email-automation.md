## Why it exists

I got frustrated with support and built something that made my own day lighter.
The repetitive part of an inbox is not the answering — it is the reading, sorting,
and deciding what deserves attention first.

## What it does

It automates the repetitive inbox work and escalates the complex cases to whoever
should actually own them. Drafts land in the original thread; it never sends on
its own.
