## An internal practice, opened up

Using Hermes in the open turned a private workflow into a format other people
could take something from. Rather than demo a finished thing, the session shared
the real workflows — including the parts still being figured out — and then handed
the room time to build.

## How it ran

A practical case study, then co-building, then participant demos. People left with
an agent shaped around their own context rather than a copy of mine.
