## Building the room, not only the schedule

Push to Prod: Building at the Frontier is a one-day builder sprint with Anthropic and Elevation Capital for audacious products, new interfaces, frontier capabilities, and infrastructure built from India.

I am leading the initiative across the builder experience and the operational work required to move it from an idea to a room full of people ready to build.

## My focus

The work lives across several layers at once:

- shaping a clear story for the right builders;
- designing the journey from discovery and application to the day itself;
- coordinating the details that make the experience feel intentional;
- keeping communication, partners, builders, and operations aligned;
- making enough room for experimentation without losing clarity.

## A live case study

This program is currently in progress. I am treating this as a living case study and will add the program decisions, artifacts, and outcomes after the event rather than claiming results early.
