University hackathons are often held together by a small group of students learning fundraising, partnerships, operations, and community building while they are already doing the work.

The grants program paired financial help and AWS resources with operational support and the Devfolio platform. My role was to run the university program and help make that support usable for organizers rather than leaving it as an announcement.
