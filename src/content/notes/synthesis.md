The Synthesis explored a different kind of hackathon workflow: agents could register projects and assist with parts of evaluation, while people retained the final decisions.

I helped support the program online. It connected directly to a question present in my internal systems work too: where can an agent create leverage, and where should the boundary remain deliberately human?
