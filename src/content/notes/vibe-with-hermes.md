My work with Hermes started as a practical way to make support, research, and community operations easier to navigate. The interesting part was not the chatbot—it was encoding the repeated decisions around the work.

Vibe With Hermes Agent brought that practice into a room. I shared the workflows I use at Devfolio, then builders created agents around their own context and showed what they had made.

The meetup reinforced something I keep returning to: a tool becomes much more useful when people can see how it fits into real work, change it, and make it their own.
