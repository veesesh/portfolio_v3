## Programs are systems made of people

A strong builder program is more than its venue or application form. It is the full chain of expectations, communication, support, energy, and small operational decisions that help someone move from curious to participating.

My work crosses that whole chain: community context, program positioning, applicant experience, support, on-ground execution, and the systems that help a small team keep up.

## At Devfolio

I have contributed to builder initiatives across formats and geographies:

- **Build India 2026**, a builder-first AI sprint focused on products made for Indian realities;
- **Push to Prod with Genspark & Claude** in Singapore;
- **Warpspeed 2025**, a 24-hour agentic AI hackathon;
- **ETHIndiaVilla**, a smaller and more intentional builder format;
- online support for **ETHDenver 2026** and **The Synthesis**.

I also ran the university program for **Devfolio Student Hackathon Grants with AWS**, helping student organizers access grants, resources, operational support, and the Devfolio platform.

## Community experiments

For **Vibe With Hermes Agent**, I was both organizer and speaker. The format combined a practical case study, co-building, and participant demos around personalized agent workflows.

Before Devfolio, I grew through student communities: leading Hackerabad, volunteering at CodeDay Hyderabad, and eventually running its third regional edition. Those rooms taught me that the best operations are often invisible—the builders simply feel that they belong and can begin.
